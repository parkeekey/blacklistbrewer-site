Location questions

Good to know

Where exactly is Blacklist Brewer?

On Thalang Road in Phuket Old Town, in front of the tunnel to Dibuk Road that connects to the Shell gas station, beside the ORTA Old Town souvenir shop. Coordinates 7.884778, 98.388806; Plus code V9MQ+WG3 Phuket.

Is Blacklist Brewer a brewery, bar, or pub?

No. Blacklist Brewer is a small walk-up specialty coffee stand. It is not a brewery, bar, or pub, and it is not affiliated with Blacklist Brewing or any other business with a similar name.

What are the coordinates and Plus code?

Coordinates: 7.884778, 98.388806. Plus code: V9MQ+WG3 Phuket, Mueang Phuket District, Phuket, Thailand.

When is Blacklist Brewer open?

Tuesday to Saturday, 18:00–22:00, filter and V60 only. Sunday afternoon until 22:00 during Phuket Walking Street, with a fuller menu.

How do I get there?

Walk along Thalang Road in Phuket Old Town to the Dibuk Road tunnel near the Shell gas station. The stand is in front of the tunnel, beside the ORTA Old Town souvenir shop.
